About me

I see design as a bridge between vision and function which starts with the right questions and ends with clear, useful solutions that serve both people and business. Over the last two years, I've been focused on learning by doing: mapping user flows, shaping design systems and improving how people interact with digital products. Along the way, I've picked up low-code tools and front-end basics to better connect design with implementation.

Outside of work, I keep a steady routine. I run, eat with intention, recover in the sauna and make time to reflect - it helps me stay clear-headed and focused. I'm also learning Spanish daily, which reminds me what it's like to be a learner and stay patient with progress.

Right now, I'm building Metis, a language flashcard app, with my talented younger brother who does a lot of coding. It all started from my own frustration with messy learning tools. Now we're trying to make studying languages more direct, more efficient and more enjoyable integrating AI to cut down the noise and let people focus on learning and progress itself.

This September, I'll start studying UX Design at University of the Arts London. I'm also learning Next.js and Tailwind CSS to prototype more tangibly and speak the language of engineers (at least a bit). I want to grow into a designer who makes an impact and inspire others.
