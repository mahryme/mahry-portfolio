Designing a Unified Personal Account

Removed third‑party friction, brought everything under one roof, and set the stage for long‑term growth

My role

Timeline

Impact

Solo Product Designer

6 months (H2 2024)

–65 % Support Tickets

Overview

Context

The Instapreneurs is a subscription‑based learning platform for junior to mid‑level marketing and SMM specialists. As a lean startup still honing its market fit, we needed to improve the user experience. Our learners want to boost their income, manage their time better, and pick up new skills—and their personal account is the gateway to all our courses and UGC library.

Problem

Because our personal‑account lived inside Kwiga’s third‑party interface, learners faced a disjointed, confusing experience, we couldn’t track how they used the product, and support tickets surged—especially around playback issues, pricing confusion, and cancellations.

Objective
  • Unify the experience by migrating off Kwiga into a single, cohesive personal-account interface
  • Reduce support load by clarifying navigation, subscription status, and content access
  • Boost engagement through improved discovery: search, filters, playlists and bite-sized “10-minute” lectures
  • Enable analytics to capture user behaviour and preferences for personalised content
My role

As the solo Product Designer, I owned research, information architecture, UI design, and design‑system implementation through to developer hand‑off.
